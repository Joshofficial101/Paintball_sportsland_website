Review the official field rules and complete the required waiver before arriving.

Field Rules

  1. Goggles / Mask

    Wear your goggles at all times unless a referee gives you permission to remove them. If you are unsure, keep them on and ask a referee. Keep your goggles on after elimination and after a game until a referee gives the all-clear.

  2. Warnings and Goggle Violations

    The first violation receives a verbal warning. The second violation requires sitting out one game. A third violation results in ejection for the day, with no refund. Eye injuries can be serious—always wear your goggles.

  3. Barrel Sleeves

    Keep your barrel sleeve on at all times except while actively playing.

  4. Game Play

    A hit eliminates you from the game; paint splatter does not count as a hit. Follow the referee’s instructions about boundaries and leaving the field. After you are eliminated, stay silent, keep your goggles on, and put your barrel sleeve on.

    No arguing, blind firing, cheating, climbing trees, overkills, moving or altering bunkers, or shooting animals.

  5. Surrender Rule

    You may surrender at any time for any reason. Do not bunker another player. At approximately 20 feet or closer, ask the player to surrender instead of shooting.

  6. Friendly Fire

    Friendly fire counts.

  7. Calling Yourself Out

    Once you call yourself out, you stay out for that game.

  8. Sportsmanship

    Be friendly, be kind, be safe, and have fun.

Rental Equipment Rules

  • Follow staff instructions for using your rental marker and filling the hopper.
  • If you accidentally fire with the barrel sleeve on or have broken paint in the hopper, ask staff to have the marker cleaned.
  • Do not pick paintballs up from the ground. Doing so can result in the loss of your rental marker.
  • No outside paintballs are allowed.
  • A 20 oz. CO₂ tank on a rental marker provides approximately 1,000 shots.

Age & Parent/Guardian Requirements

Players ages 10–17 must have the waiver signed by a parent or guardian before playing. Children under age 10 may not play games at Paintball Sports Land, but may use the target range area for free practice. A parent or legal guardian must be present when a minor’s waiver is signed.
