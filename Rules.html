Before You Play

Review the official field rules and complete the required waiver before arriving.

Required Waiver

Every paintball participant must sign a Release of Liability form before playing. Download, print, and complete the waiver before game day.

Field Rules

  1. Wear your mask at all times.
  2. Keep your barrel cover on at all times unless you are actively playing.
  3. Follow the surrender rule: do not shoot another player within 20 feet.
  4. Do not blind fire. Look where you are shooting.
  5. Do not move bunkers or climb trees.
  6. One hit eliminates you for that round, including hits to your gun or mask. When eliminated, raise your hand and walk back to the safe zone.
  7. Eliminated players may not talk.
  8. Physical contact between players is not allowed.
  9. Safe zones are staging areas. You may remove your mask inside them, but all guns must have barrel covers on.
  10. Rental players may not use paintballs that are on the ground.

Age & Parent/Guardian Requirements

Players ages 10–17 must have the waiver signed by a parent or guardian before playing. Children under age 10 may not play games at Paintball Sports Land, but may use the target range area for free practice. A parent or legal guardian must be present when a minor’s waiver is signed.
