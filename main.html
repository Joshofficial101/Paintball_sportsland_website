Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you plan your visit to Paintball Sports Land.

Will paint wash out?

Yes, paint washes out of clothing, but we recommend washing your clothes as soon as possible.

What should I wear?

We recommend wearing older clothes and long pants.

Can a group of 10 or more players join walk-on paintball?

Yes. Groups of 10 or more can join walk-on paintball. We recommend calling ahead to help guarantee rentals for your group.

Do you offer Airsoft rentals?

Yes, but rental quantities are limited. Reserve ahead of time to help guarantee an Airsoft gun.
