Field safety

Velocity & Face Protection Rules

All players must meet these field limits and safety-equipment requirements before playing.

Velocity Limits

Regular AEGs
400 FPS with .20 BBs
Snipers
500 FPS with .20 BBs
Sniper engagement range
Minimum 100 feet
Rate of fire
Maximum 25 RPS

Face Mask Rules

  • Players under 18 must wear a full-face mask.
  • Players 18 and older may wear a two-piece face mask.
  • Eye protection must be full seal; regular safety glasses are not allowed.
  • A lower face mask must be worn with eye protection.
